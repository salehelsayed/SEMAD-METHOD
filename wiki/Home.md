# SEMAD Method Wiki

Welcome to the SEMAD Method Wiki - your comprehensive guide to the Systematic Enterprise Method for Agile Development.

## What is SEMAD?

SEMAD is a revolutionary framework that combines AI agents with software development methodologies to create a systematic, scalable approach to building enterprise-grade applications. It transforms traditional development workflows by introducing intelligent agents that handle specific roles in the development lifecycle.
